### 警告框

> 参考：[警告框 | markdown格式总结 | Obsidian 教程](https://fdscend.github.io/obsidian_tutorial/markdown%E6%A0%BC%E5%BC%8F%E6%80%BB%E7%BB%93#%E8%AD%A6%E5%91%8A%E6%A1%86)

> [!multi-column]
>
> > [!note] note
>
> > [!abstract] abstract, summary, tldr
>
> > [!info] info
>
> > [!todo] todo
>
> > [!tip] tip, hint, important
>
> > [!success] success, check, done
>

>
> > [!warning] warning, caution, attention

> > [!failure] failure, fail, missing

> > [!danger] danger, error

> > [!bug] bug
>
> > [!example] example
>
> > [!quote] quote, cite
